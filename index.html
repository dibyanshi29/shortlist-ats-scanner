    Why this layout passes ATS

    1. One column, read top to bottom. No tables, text boxes or sidebars that parsers scramble.
    2. Standard section headings: Summary, Skills, Experience, Projects, Education.
    3. Contact details in the body, not the page header or footer.
    4. Plain fonts (Calibri/Arial) and real text. No icons, photos, skill bars or charts.
    5. Job title, company and dates on one line, dates as “Mon YYYY”.
    6. Submit .docx unless the posting asks for PDF.
